# Project Context

## Identity

- Name: Gridcore
- Platform: Pebble Time 2 (Emery) and Pebble Round 2 (Gabbro)
- Runtime: Pebble SDK 4.33.1, C watchface, Clay phone settings

## Purpose

Make a legible digital clock whose chamfered segmented numerals have visible color and depth at native watch resolution.

## Constraints

- Both platform binaries must ship in one PBW.
- Date options are Off, YYYY-MM-DD, MM/DD/YYYY, and DD/MM/YYYY.
- The watch's 12-hour or 24-hour setting controls the hour.
- Motion is optional and must settle quickly.
- Native captures are required before store media or release claims.

## Out of Scope

- Physical-watch behavior and public store acceptance remain unverified.
